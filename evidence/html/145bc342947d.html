Join the Echobae Affiliate Program

Earn commissions by sharing Echobae with your audience. Joining is free and open to everyone.

How It Works

1. Sign Up

Click Join the Affiliate Program to register.

Once approved, you’ll receive your unique affiliate link and discount code.

2. Share

Promote your affiliate link through:

  • Social media

  • Your website or blog

  • Communities and content channels

  • Direct recommendations to your audience

3. Earn Commission

Earn up to 10% commission on qualifying sales made through your affiliate link or discount code.

You can also earn 2% second-tier commission when someone joins the Echobae Affiliate Program through your referral and generates qualifying sales.

4. Get Paid

Commissions are paid within two months after an order is successfully completed.

Cancelled, unpaid, or incomplete orders are not eligible for commission.

Why Join Echobae?

Up to 10% Commission

Earn commission every time a customer purchases through your affiliate link or code.

2% Second-Tier Earnings

Refer new affiliates and earn 2% commission on their qualifying sales.

This second-tier commission remains active for as long as the referred affiliate stays eligible in the program.

Long-Term Earning Potential

Your content and affiliate links can continue generating commissions after they are published.

No Inventory or Shipping

Echobae handles products, fulfillment, shipping, and customer service. You focus on promotion.

Reliable Tracking & Payments

Track referrals and commissions through your affiliate dashboard, with payments processed according to our commission schedule.

Affiliate Program vs. Influencer Program

Affiliate Program

Open to everyone.

Share your affiliate link or discount code and earn commission from qualifying sales. No strict application requirements.

Influencer Program

Designed for selected creators and influencers.

Applications are reviewed by the Echobae team. Approved creators may receive opportunities for long-term partnerships, product reviews, testing, and sponsored campaigns.

FAQ

How much commission can I earn?

You can earn up to 10% on qualifying sales through your affiliate link or discount code.

What is the second-tier commission?

If someone becomes an Echobae affiliate through your referral, you can earn 2% of qualifying sales generated by that affiliate.

When are commissions paid?

Commissions are paid within two months after the related order is successfully completed.

Which orders qualify?

Only completed and paid orders qualify. Cancelled, unpaid, refunded, or incomplete orders are excluded.

How do I join?

Click Join the Affiliate Program, complete the registration, and get your unique affiliate link.
