Shipping & Delivery

At Echobae, we want every order to arrive safely, privately, and with clear shipping information from the beginning.

All orders are shipped in discreet packaging and include tracking information once dispatched. Available shipping methods and shipping costs depend on the destination, product weight, package size, and selected delivery option.

Discreet, Tracked, and Reliable

Discreet Packaging: All orders are shipped in plain, unbranded packaging with no product information shown on the outside.

Order Tracking: Tracking information will be provided after your order has been shipped.

Shipping Updates: You may receive shipping updates by email depending on the carrier and order status.

Reliable Delivery: We work with established international logistics providers and local delivery partners depending on the destination.

Shipping Methods

We currently offer two main shipping options for eligible orders.

Standard Shipping

Standard Shipping is the more economical option for customers who are comfortable with a longer delivery time.

Estimated shipping time: approximately 30–35 days after dispatch.

Delivery time may vary depending on the destination, customs clearance, local carrier processing, and remote-area delivery conditions.

Express Shipping

Express Shipping is the faster option for customers who prefer shorter delivery times.

Estimated shipping time: approximately 10–14 business days after dispatch.

Depending on the destination and shipping route, final delivery may be completed by FedEx, UPS, DHL, or other local logistics partners.

Shipping Rates

Shipping costs are calculated based on your destination, product weight, package size, and selected shipping method.

The final available shipping options and shipping cost will be shown at checkout before payment.

Additional charges may apply to remote areas or oversized packages.

Production & Processing Time

Some Echobae dolls require production or preparation based on the selected configuration.

Production and processing time are separate from shipping time.

Shipping time begins after the doll has completed production, quality inspection, packaging, and has been handed over to the carrier.

Customized orders may require additional processing time.

Shipping availability may vary by country, region, postal code, product weight, and local shipping restrictions.

If your destination is not available during checkout, please contact us before placing your order.

Delivery Times

Estimated delivery times after dispatch are:

Standard Shipping: approximately 30–35 days

Express Shipping: approximately 10–14 business days

These estimates do not include production or order preparation time.

Actual delivery may take longer due to customs clearance, holidays, carrier delays, severe weather, remote-area delivery, or other circumstances outside our control.

Customs & Taxes

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, VAT, GST, or other local charges depending on the destination.

Any fees charged by local customs authorities or delivery carriers are determined by the destination country and may vary by order.

If you are unsure whether additional customs charges may apply, please check your local import requirements before placing an order.

Factory Photos Before Shipping

Before shipment, your completed doll may undergo a final quality inspection.

For selected orders, factory photos may be provided before dispatch so you can review the appearance and selected configuration before the product is packed and shipped.

This helps confirm that the order is ready for delivery.

Address Information

Please make sure your shipping information is complete and accurate before placing your order.

Your order should include:

  • Full recipient name

  • Complete street address

  • City

  • State or province

  • Postal code

  • Country

  • Valid phone number

  • Valid email address

Incorrect or incomplete shipping information may result in delays or additional delivery charges.

Shipping Delays

All delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed arrival dates.

Shipping may take longer during peak seasons, public holidays, customs inspections, extreme weather, or unexpected carrier disruptions.

If a significant shipping delay occurs, please contact us with your order number and tracking information so we can assist with checking the shipment status.

Damaged or Missing Packages

If your package arrives with visible damage, please take photos or video of the outer packaging before opening it.

If the product is damaged during transportation, please contact us and provide your order number, shipping label, packaging photos, and photos or video of the damaged product.

If tracking has not updated for an extended period or the package appears to be missing, please contact us so we can assist with checking the shipment with the carrier.

Clothing, Wigs & Accessories

Clothing, wigs, jewelry, accessories, and props shown in product images are not automatically included unless they are specifically listed as included on the product page.

Please review the product details and selected configuration before placing your order.

Contact Us

If you have questions about shipping availability, delivery time, or shipping costs, please contact Echobae customer service before placing your order.

Please provide your country, postal code, and product model so we can check the available shipping options for your destination.
